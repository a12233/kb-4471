parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

A day-first tenant writes 07/04 for 7 April, and the parser reads 4 July. These reports come in by app, by email and through booking-v2’s form. This is the fix that is queued.

hint: booking-v3’s form tells people to type the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint says, and day and month swap. Only reports sent through the booking-v3 form, at booking-v3 tenants, have this cause. The parser fix does not touch it: the form has to print the tenant’s own order.

one-day-move: dates land a day early or late

Not a flip. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” The date moves by one day, which points at time zones, not at day and month order. The desk read these in the report bodies (desk-notes.json). By tenant they were filed under parser, so the parser fix would have closed them unfixed.

How the reports are counted

  • One report per id. The export pages overlap, so some reports come twice.
  • The cause comes from the tenant’s locale and booking form, not from intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the guess is often wrong: compare “tagged as” below.
  • A report the desk read as a one-day move counts there, wherever its tenant would file it.
