parser: the shared parser read dates month first

A day-first tenant writes 07/04 for 7 April, and the parser read 4 July. These reports came in by app, by email and through booking-v2’s form. Fixed: the parser now reads dd/mm for EU locales.

hint: booking-v3’s form told people to type the month first

booking-v3 printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, then read the date the tenant’s way, day first. People typed what the hint said, and day and month swapped. Only reports sent through the booking-v3 form, at booking-v3 tenants, have this cause. The table marks it “no ticket of its own”; it has one now, #2. Won’t fix: the form is retired, so no new reports can come in this way.

One-day move: dates land a day early or late

Not a flip. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” The date moves by one day, which points at time zones, not at day and month order. The desk read these in the report bodies (desk-notes.json). By tenant they are counted under parser. The parser fix does not explain them, so #3 stays open until someone checks them against it.

Closing #4471 does not close the one-day moves. Their ticket stays open.

Since April

How the reports are counted

  • One report per id. The export pages overlap, so some reports come twice.
  • The cause comes from the tenant’s locale and booking form, not from intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the guess is often wrong: compare “tagged as” in the table.
  • The counts are the desk’s own view, as exported.

Lessons

  • A page can say one thing while its own table says another. Read the table.
  • Merging on subject (decision 0001) put three problems in one ticket. A subject names the symptom, and all three share it: the wrong day.
